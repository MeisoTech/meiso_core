Meiso CJK16 is a fixed-record format conversion of GNU Unifont 17.0.05.
The glyph designs are unchanged; 8-pixel rows are padded to a 16-bit record.
Conversion: 2026-09-08. Source and reproducible converter: make_overlay_font.py.
Upstream: https://unifoundry.com/unifont/
